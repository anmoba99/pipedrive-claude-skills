# Healthcare and medtech (B2B)

Object map: deal = sale to a hospital, clinic group or lab; person = clinician, procurement, administrator; org = facility or group; product = device, consumable, software, service contract.
Pipeline: Lead > Clinical interest > Demo / evaluation > Value analysis committee > Tender / procurement > Contract > Installed.
Qualify (MEDDICC): clinical champion, economic buyer, committee dates, budget cycle, tender rules, competitor installed base, evaluation criteria.
Risk signals: committee date passed with no decision, champion leaves, tender published with specs that favor competitor, no procurement contact after evaluation.
Upsell signals: new department or site, consumables volume growth, service contract renewal, software add-on.
Custom fields worth adding: Committee date, Tender ID, Tender deadline, Budget year, Clinical champion, Installed competitor, Contract end date.
Best skills: pd-call-prep (stakeholder map), pd-pipeline-review forecast (long cycles), pd-account-health renewals, pd-win-loss (tender losses).
Watch-outs: never put patient information in Pipedrive notes or chat. Follow local healthcare compliance rules on interactions with clinicians.
