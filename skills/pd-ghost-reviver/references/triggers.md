# Why now: triggers and openers

## Windows by reason class

Days after the loss when a new conversation usually makes sense. Used by the script; adjust per industry (pd-industry pack) and say so.

| Class | Window | Why |
|---|---|---|
| timing | 90 to 270 | "Not now" usually means a quarter or two |
| budget | 300 to 420 | Next budget cycle |
| competitor | 300 to 400, again 695 to 765 | First and second contract anniversary |
| no_decision | 120 to 365 | New priorities, new people |
| went_silent | 90 to 365 | One honest restart, then stop |
| price | 180 to 400 | New pricing, smaller scope, or their budget grew |
| product_gap | 120 to 540 | Only if the gap was closed: ask the user |
| other | 180 to 365 | Low priority |
| bad_fit | never | |

## Data triggers

- Contact now linked to another organization: a warm intro, they know the product.
- Organization updated in the last 60 days with no open deal: someone at the account is active again; ask the user what changed.
- A date in the notes ("after the summer", "in Q2"): that date has passed. Strongest trigger, use their words.

## Openers (one per draft)

| Trigger | Opener idea |
|---|---|
| Timing came around | "You mentioned <their timing words>. Is that still the plan?" |
| Budget cycle | "New budget year at <org>. Worth a 15 minute look at <their goal>?" |
| Competitor anniversary | "Your <competitor or current setup> agreement is likely up for review around now." Only name the competitor if notes name it. |
| Moved company | "Congrats on the move to <new org>. What we did for <old goal> may fit there too." |
| Went silent | "Closing the loop on <topic>. Should I keep this open or close it?" |
| Product gap closed | "You needed <gap>. We have it now." Only if the user confirms. |

## Rules

- One specific memory from the old conversation per draft. No memory: skip the deal.
- Never fake familiarity, urgency or scarcity. Never imply a meeting that did not happen.
- No prices, discounts or terms the user did not give.
