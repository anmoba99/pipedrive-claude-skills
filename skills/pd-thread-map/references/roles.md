# Buying roles

Tag each person with one role. Evidence first, title second, unknown when neither.

| Role | Evidence in notes and activity |
|---|---|
| Champion | Pushes the deal internally, shares inside info, sets up meetings with others, "I'll present this to the board", most two-way touches |
| Decision maker | Signs or approves budget, "needs sign-off from", joined late for pricing or contract, CFO, owner, director titles |
| Influencer / evaluator | Runs the trial, compares options, asks technical or detailed questions, joins demos |
| User | Will use the product or service day to day, asks how it works for them |
| Gatekeeper | Procurement, legal, IT security, assistant; controls access or process, not the decision |
| Blocker | Raises the same objection repeatedly, prefers a competitor or status quo, "not convinced", meetings cancelled by them |
| Unknown | Linked to the deal, no evidence |

## Rules

- One quote per tag, under 15 words, with the note date. Or a pattern: "3 of 4 meetings set up by her".
- Title alone gives a weak tag: write "(by title)".
- A champion with no touch in 30 days: flag "champion quiet".
- No decision maker after the stage where wins usually had one: flag "no decision maker".
- Never write a role into Pipedrive without the safe-write change set.
- Never guess personal facts (age, health, politics) about a contact. Roles only.
