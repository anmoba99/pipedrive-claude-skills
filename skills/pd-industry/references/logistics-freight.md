# Logistics and freight

Object map: deal = lane, contract or spot shipment opportunity; person = shipper contact, procurement; org = shipper or forwarder; product = service (FTL, LTL, air, sea, warehousing).
Pipeline: Lead > Discovery (lanes, volumes) > Rate request > Quote sent > Trial shipment > Contract > Active.
Qualify: lanes (origin, destination), monthly volume, current carrier, pain (price, reliability, capacity), contract vs spot, tender cycle date.
Risk signals: quote older than rate validity, trial shipment with no feedback, tender date passed, volume drop on active account.
Upsell signals: new lanes, seasonal peaks, warehousing need, cross-border expansion, carrier issue at competitor.
Custom fields worth adding: Lanes, Monthly volume, Mode, Rate valid until, Contract end date, Tender date, Current carrier.
Best skills: pd-follow-up (quote chasers), pd-account-health renewals (contract expiry), pd-pipeline-review, pd-lead-qualifier (rate request completeness).
Watch-outs: rates change fast; never reuse old rates from notes in a draft without a check.
