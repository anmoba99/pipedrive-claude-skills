# Agencies and consulting

Object map: deal = project or retainer; person = client contact, decision maker; org = client company; product = service line (strategy, design, development, retainer hours).
Pipeline: Inquiry > Discovery call > Brief received > Proposal sent > Pitch / presentation > Negotiation > Won. Retainers: separate Renewal pipeline.
Qualify (CHAMP): challenge and business goal, budget range, decision maker and process, timeline or launch date, fit with your services, pitch competitors.
Risk signals: proposal sent with no reply in 7 days, brief vague after two calls, budget not discussed by proposal stage, procurement or pitch list appears late, scope growing without value change.
Upsell signals: new markets or brands mentioned, retainer hours exceeded, adjacent service asked about, client contact changes company (follow them).
Custom fields worth adding: Service line, Budget range, Pitch date, Retainer end date, Monthly retainer value, Competing agencies, Referral source.
Best skills: pd-follow-up (proposal chasers), pd-lead-qualifier (CHAMP), pd-account-health renewals, pd-win-loss (pitch losses).
Watch-outs: proposals live in docs; paste the key numbers into the deal value and products so forecasts work.
