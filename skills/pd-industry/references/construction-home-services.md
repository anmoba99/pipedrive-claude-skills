# Construction and home services

Object map: deal = job (installation, renovation, repair, project); person = homeowner or site contact; org = builder, property manager, business client; product = service package or materials.
Pipeline: Inquiry > Site visit booked > Site visit done > Quote sent > Follow-up > Won > Scheduled > Completed > Review requested.
Qualify: job type and size, address and access, budget, desired start date, decision makers, permits needed, financing.
Risk signals: site visit done with no quote in 3 days, quote with no reply in 5 days, start date approaching with no deposit, weather or seasonal delay, competitor quote mentioned.
Upsell signals: maintenance contract, adjacent job (roof plus gutters), referral from finished job, seasonal service due.
Custom fields worth adding: Job type, Site address, Site visit date, Quote valid until, Deposit paid (yes/no), Start date, Season, Review requested (yes/no).
Best skills: pd-lead-qualifier (job fit before site visit), pd-follow-up (quote chasers), pd-morning-brief, pd-win-loss (price vs timing losses).
Watch-outs: high lead volume from forms; use pd-lead-qualifier to sort, pd-data-hygiene for duplicate homeowners.
