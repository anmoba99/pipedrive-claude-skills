# Financial services and insurance

Object map: deal = policy, product sale or advisory mandate; person = client; org = household or company; product = policy type or financial product.
Pipelines: New business: Lead > Fact-find > Needs analysis > Proposal > Application > Underwriting / approval > Issued. Renewals: separate pipeline by renewal date.
Qualify: need type, current cover or provider, renewal date of current policy, budget or premium range, decision makers in the household, compliance steps completed.
Risk signals: application stuck in underwriting, renewal within 30 days with no contact, client asked for competitor quote, missing compliance step.
Upsell signals: life events (marriage, child, house, business growth), cover gap found in fact-find, policy bundle opportunity.
Custom fields worth adding: Product type, Premium, Renewal date, Current provider, Household, Compliance check done (yes/no), Next review date.
Best skills: pd-account-health renewals, pd-follow-up (renewal and review reminders), pd-morning-brief, pd-pipeline-review.
Watch-outs: never store account numbers, government IDs or health details in notes or chat. Follow your regulator's record-keeping rules; Claude notes are summaries, not official records.
