# Events and hospitality

Object map: deal = booking or event (conference, wedding, group stay, corporate offsite); person = planner, client; org = company or agency; product = room block, venue, catering package.
Pipeline: Inquiry / RFP > Requirements > Site visit > Proposal > Hold (tentative) > Contract signed > Deposit paid > Event done.
Qualify: event date and flexibility, guest count, budget per head, room nights, decision deadline, competing venues.
Risk signals: tentative hold expiring, proposal with no reply in 5 days, guest count dropping, deposit overdue, date conflict with another deal.
Upsell signals: annual event, extra nights, upgraded package, add-on services, agency with multiple clients.
Custom fields worth adding: Event date, Guest count, Room nights, Hold expiry date, Budget per head, Deposit due date, Event type.
Best skills: pd-morning-brief (hold expiries), pd-follow-up, pd-pipeline-review (by event month), pd-ask ("any bookings on 14 March?" date conflict check).
Watch-outs: date conflicts are the biggest risk; keep Event date filled on every deal.
