# Education and training

Object map: B2C: deal = enrollment, person = student or parent. B2B: deal = training contract, org = company, product = course or program with seats.
Pipelines: Enrollment: Inquiry > Info session > Application > Interview / assessment > Offer > Deposit > Enrolled. Corporate: Lead > Needs analysis > Proposal > Pilot > Contract.
Qualify: program fit, start date or cohort, funding source (self, employer, grant), prerequisites met, decision maker (student, parent, HR, L&D).
Risk signals: application started not finished in 7 days, offer with no deposit by deadline, cohort under minimum size, funding not confirmed.
Upsell signals: alumni advanced course, company sending more staff, team-wide program interest.
Custom fields worth adding: Program, Cohort start, Funding source, Application status, Deposit deadline, Seats (B2B).
Best skills: pd-morning-brief (application nudges), pd-follow-up, pd-pipeline-review (cohort fill by program), pd-lead-qualifier.
Watch-outs: minors' data needs extra care; keep only what enrollment needs.
