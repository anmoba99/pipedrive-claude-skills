# Manufacturing and wholesale

Object map: deal = quote or order opportunity; person = buyer, engineer, procurement; org = customer, distributor; product = SKU or product family with price and quantity.
Pipeline: Inquiry > Requirements > Sample / spec check > Quote sent > Negotiation > PO received > Delivered. Distributors: Onboarding pipeline.
Qualify: annual volume, target price, spec or certification needs, delivery schedule, current supplier, payment terms, decision process (engineering plus procurement).
Risk signals: quote older than its validity date, sample sent with no feedback in 14 days, price objection with no counteroffer, repeat customer past their usual reorder date.
Upsell signals: new product line, new plant or location, volume increase, quality issue with current supplier, seasonal peak coming.
Custom fields worth adding: Annual volume, Target price, Quote valid until, Incoterms, Payment terms, Reorder cycle (days), Last order date, Territory.
Best skills: pd-account-health renewals (reorder radar: last order date + reorder cycle), pd-follow-up (quote chasers), pd-pipeline-review, pd-data-hygiene (distributor duplicates).
Watch-outs: use deal products for line items so value is correct; ERP data is outside Pipedrive.
