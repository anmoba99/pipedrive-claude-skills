# Nonprofits

Object map: deal = gift, pledge, sponsorship or grant; person = donor; org = foundation, company sponsor; product = campaign or program.
Pipelines: Major gifts: Identified > Qualified > Cultivation > Ask made > Pledged > Received > Thanked. Grants: Research > Letter of inquiry > Proposal > Decision > Awarded > Reporting.
Qualify: capacity, affinity to the cause, past giving, connection (board, volunteer), timing, preferred program.
Risk signals: pledged but not received in 30 days, donor not thanked in 7 days, lapsed donor (no gift in 12+ months), grant report deadline close.
Upsell signals: increased gift, recurring giving, matching gift from employer, legacy giving interest, board invitation.
Custom fields worth adding: Gift type, Campaign, Pledge date, Received date, Thanked (yes/no), Last gift date, Grant deadline, Report due date.
Best skills: pd-morning-brief (thank-yous, lapsed donors), pd-follow-up (stewardship emails), pd-pipeline-review digest for the board, pd-account-health (donor health).
Watch-outs: donors expect privacy; keep notes respectful and factual.
