# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or sharing someone else's private information.
- Never post real customer or CRM data in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through [GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/anmoba99/pipedrive-claude-skills/security/advisories/new). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders.
